**Föreningen äger sin data - the association owns its data.**

Open BRF is an open source, self-hostable platform for Swedish housing cooperatives (bostadsrättsföreningar, "BRF"). One system for the whole association: the board, and everyone who lives in the building.

**For residents.** News and documents, booking the laundry room, the sauna or the guest apartment, events, issue reports with photos, key orders, subletting applications, motions to the general meeting, the apartment binder, and chat with the neighbours. Everyone in the household gets an account of their own.

**For the board.** The statutory member and apartment registers, mailings and the association's public website, with Swedish law built in: the Housing Cooperatives Act and GDPR, with purging, access reports, and protected personal data masked everywhere.

**No lock-in.** AGPL-3.0, no binding period, and self-hosting is free, forever.

> **Pre-release.** A pilot in a real association starts in December 2026, and the first public release is planned for Q1 2027. Nothing here has run a housing cooperative yet.
